# Horizon compatibility overrides

The generator extracts name, job, and level tables from
`Mr-Sithel/HorizonXI-Dynamis-Mobdb`. Its commit is pinned in
`tools/mob_data_sources.json` and recorded in generated `source.json`.

Source: https://github.com/Mr-Sithel/HorizonXI-Dynamis-Mobdb

The Horizon edits are by Mr-Sithel, based on ThornyFFXI's MobDB data.
The original MIT license is included as `LICENSE`. Generated files contain only
compatibility overrides; detection, damage adjustments, ranks, and immunities
come from XIUI's LandSandBoat snapshot.
